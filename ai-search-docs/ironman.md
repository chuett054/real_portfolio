# Cole's Ironman training

Cole Huetten is training to become an Ironman (2026 to present). An Ironman is a 2.4 mile swim, a 112 mile bike, and a 26.2 mile run, all in one race.

## Why

Cole doesn't really know why he got into the sport, but some of his earliest memories are watching his dad at his races, so that probably contributes to it. He has never liked endurance sports either, so it's a bit strange.

## The goal: IRONMAN Copenhagen in 2027

Cole's goal is to go back to his study abroad location in Copenhagen, Denmark to complete his first full-length Ironman, IRONMAN Copenhagen, in 2027. Copenhagen is very flat, so it is considered an easy bike and run compared to other Ironman locations.

## Race schedule

- November 15, 2026: first triathlon, international distance, in Tampa, Florida. Finished.
- April 4, 2027: IRONMAN 70.3 Oceanside in Oceanside, California. A half Ironman: 1.2 mile swim, 56 mile bike, 13.1 mile run. Upcoming.
- 2027: IRONMAN Copenhagen in Copenhagen, Denmark. A full Ironman: 2.4 mile swim, 112 mile bike, 26.2 mile run. Cole's goal race. Upcoming.

## First triathlon: Tampa, November 15, 2026

Cole's first ever triathlon was an international distance race (a quarter Ironman) in Tampa, Florida on November 15, 2026: a 0.5 mile swim, a 20 mile bike, and a 6.2 mile run.

His goals were to finish, swim in under 20 minutes, bike in under 1 hour, and run in under 1 hour.

His splits were a 17:18 swim (goal hit), a 1:02:11 bike (a slight miss), and a 58:58 run (goal hit). His total time was 2:22:48.

He finished 2nd out of 8 participants in his age group (Male 20-24), which hurts even more because first place was only 6 minutes faster.
