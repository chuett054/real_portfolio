# Cole at Cloudflare

Cole Huetten was a Product Manager Intern at Cloudflare in San Francisco during Summer 2026, on the AI Search team.

## What AI Search is

AI Search is Cloudflare's managed search and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) product. You connect it to your data, such as files in an R2 bucket or a website, and it handles parsing, chunking, embeddings, indexing, and retrieval. Developers can then add natural-language search or AI answers grounded in their own content to an app with a few lines of code.

Common use cases include support chatbots that answer from help docs, internal knowledge-base search, documentation search, product search, and assistants that answer questions about a company's own files.

## What Cole worked on

Over the summer, Cole talked to many AI Search customers, including Chick-fil-A and Thrive Capital. He worked on a redesign of the AI Search dashboard and on improving the product's accuracy with physical documents, such as scanned PDFs and images, using optical character recognition (OCR).
