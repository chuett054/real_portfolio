# Blackout Detail

Blackout Detail is a mobile car detailing company that Cole Huetten started in 2022 and still runs on a small scale today (2022 to present). The company cleans cars at the customer's home.

## Why Cole started it

Cole founded Blackout Detail during high school in order to pay for college.

## The timing

Cole started the company in 2022, before mobile detailing took off in popularity around the beginning of 2026. Google Trends shows a large spike in US searches for "mobile detailing" in early 2026.

## Growing it

At the business peak, Cole employed 6 people, owned a fleet of 3 vans and trailers, and generated profit well into six figures in just under 3 years.

He also tried to lease a shop, which took hours upon hours of cold emailing (who would ever let a minor do that). In hindsight, it was a great choice not to.

## Today

Cole hardly runs the business anymore. He still details friends' and family's cars from time to time, but the business still gets a lot of calls and website clicks from people who find it on Google.

From June to November 2026, Blackout Detail's Google Business Profile recorded 307 calls and 64 website clicks.

Calls per month: June 47, July 70, August 72, September 50, October 59, November 9 (month in progress).
Website clicks per month: June 13, July 13, August 18, September 13, October 7, November 0 (month in progress).

## Other cool and funny things

- Cole detailed 2 cars worth over $300,000 USD.
- He once crashed a car that he was detailing.
- He had a partnership with the Minnesota Vikings.
- His Google Business page got shut down multiple times because he wouldn't respond to enough people fast enough.
